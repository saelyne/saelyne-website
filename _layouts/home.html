Hi! I'm a Postdoctoral Fellow at Carnegie Mellon University's Human-Computer Interaction Institute (HCII), working with Jeff Bigham. I earned my Ph.D. from KAIST, advised by Juho Kim, with support from the NAVER PhD Fellowship and the Kwanjeong Scholarship. I've had the opportunity to intern at Adobe Research, Autodesk Research, and LG AI Research.

My research focuses on empowering both humans and AI agents to master new skills. I build user-aware, collaborative AI agents designed to assist with complex procedural tasks by leveraging diverse learning materials (e.g., documents, videos). For this purpose, I have recently been focusing on developing computer-use agents, making them controllable and flexible for users.
Research Interests Human-AI Interaction Human-Agent Collaboration Multimodal Understanding Procedural Task Learning Skill Learning
