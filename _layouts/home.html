Hi! I'm a 5th-year Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at KAIST. I am working with Juho Kim as part of KIXLAB.
My research interests in Human-Computer Interaction lie in video understanding and interaction. I leverage HCI and AI techniques to comprehend video content and build interactive technologies that empower users to create and consume video content more effectively. In particular, I aim to improve how people learn procedural tasks through instructional/how-to videos to achieve everyday tasks.
Research Interests: Human-AI Interaction; Video Interaction; Multimodal Understanding; Procedural Task Learning; Skill Learning; Knowledge Capture and Sharing
I'm on the job market mainly seeking industry research positions. Please reach out if you know of any relevant opportunities!
